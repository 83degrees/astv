This template is a centrally managed implementation aid for creating a product-local `DIAGRAM_CONVENTION_LEARNING.md`. It is not an independent governance authority.

## Purpose

This file records recurring product-local architecture-diagram conventions observed during governed diagram work.

Entries are provisional learning.

They are not:

- central governance;
- approved product architecture;
- governed contracts; or
- overrides of the Architecture Diagram Standard.

A recorded convention may be reused as a local working default only where it does not conflict with a higher authority.

## Capture Rules

Record a convention when it is:

- recurring;
- clearly deliberate; or
- explicitly reinforced during governed diagram work.

Do not record isolated accidental formatting or invent new conventions merely for convenience.

Keep entries lightweight.
